What it is
First prototype for the revised DWP staff fraud referral form (FRF). The current Enhanced Review Team (ERT) form was a tactical solution deployed rapidly during the pandemic, it is hosted on the DWP intranet and is due to be decommissioned towards the end of 2026. The ERT form will be integrated into the FRF form to move the business closer to the end goal of a single place for staff fraud referrals. The current FRF form incorrectly replicates the look and feel of GOV.UK using the crown logo and the transport font, this prototype has been styled correctly for an internal DWP service.
